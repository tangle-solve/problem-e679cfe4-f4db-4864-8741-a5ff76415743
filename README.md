### BCGT-000237: Incentive-compatible defense adoption
- **Subfield:** Selfish mining, withholding strategies and attack rationality
- **Open:** Design activation mechanisms under which miners voluntarily adopt withholding defenses despite competitors retaining existing behavior.
- **Status:** Research target since early tie-breaking defenses; deployment feasibility and adoption incentives remain distinct requirements.
- **Type:** theory
- **Why:** Defenses may fail before reaching their adoption threshold.
- **Key works:** Zhang and Preneel (2017), *Publish or Perish: A Backward-Compatible Defense Against Selfish Mining in Bitcoin*, DOI:10.1007/978-3-319-52153-4_16; Finkbeiner et al. (2025), IACR ePrint:2025/043.
